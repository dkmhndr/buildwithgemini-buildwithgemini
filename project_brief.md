# My agent: pogo-companion
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps Pokemon GO trainers optimize their GO Battle League teams, find top raid counters, and appraise PvP IVs with a live Pokemon GO catalog.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers trainer level, team (Mystic/Valor/Instinct), buddy Pokemon, current GBL league (Great/Ultra/Master), and favorite raid teams.
- Tools: Raid boss counter lookup, Fast/Charged move breakdown (EPS/DPS, energy cost), and GBL meta ranking search.
- Catalog/UI: Raid counter infographics, PvP tier lists, and Pokemon catch appraisal cards (CP, IVs, ideal movesets) rendered as A2UI cards/tables.
- Image gen: Generates shiny Pokemon encounter snapshots, AR buddy moments, and Raid Victory celebration badges.
- Sandbox: PvP IV calculator (ranking 0/15/15 stat product for Great/Ultra leagues) and raid Time-to-Win (TTW) computations.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for PvP stat-product optimization (0/15/15 IV ceilings) and raid solo/duo TTW calculations.
